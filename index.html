Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade becomes grade points, multiplied by the course credits. The total is divided by the total credits, so bigger courses count more.

What is the 4.0 scale?

A is 4.0, B is 3.0, C is 2.0, D is 1.0 and F is 0. Plus and minus grades use half-point steps where the school applies them.

What is the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?

Unweighted GPA caps at 4.0. Weighted GPA gives extra points for harder courses, so it can rise above 4.0.

How can I raise my GPA?

Earn high grades in the credits you have left, focusing on high-credit courses where improvement is most likely. Old grades stay in the average, so it takes time.

What is a semester GPA?

The GPA for one term only. Cumulative GPA combines all terms so far, weighted by credits.

Do grade scales vary by country?

Yes. The 4.0 scale is common in the United States, but other countries use different systems, so always check your own school's scale.
